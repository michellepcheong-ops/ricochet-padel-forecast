How this call is calculated +
This is a heuristic rain-risk estimate, not an official probability. NEA publishes categorical forecasts rather than a point-specific "% chance of rain" for Orchard Road. For the next 1–2 hours, this page gives more weight to the nearest NEA 2-hour forecast area and nearby rain gauges. For hours 3–4, it gives progressively more weight to the broader Central-region 24-hour outlook. A "thundery showers" forecast therefore raises risk without automatically becoming 90%. The air-quality advisory combines 24-hour PSI guidance with NEA's 1-hour PM2.5 guide for immediate outdoor activity.
